Introduction

Digital Document Landscape

  • 🌐 Digital documents are widely used in banking, education, healthcare and government systems.
  • ⚠️ Document forgery and identity theft are increasing rapidly.
  • 🗄️ Traditional verification systems rely on centralized databases.
  • 🔓 These systems are vulnerable to data breaches and tampering.
  • ✅ Therefore, a secure and intelligent verification system is required.
02 / 13
Problem Statement

Challenges in Verification

  • 🐌 Manual document verification is slow and error-prone.
  • ⛓️ Centralized systems create a single point of failure.
  • 🤖 Traditional systems cannot detect advanced fraud patterns.
  • 🔍 Lack of transparency in verification records.
  • 🛡️ Need for automated and tamper-proof verification platform.

Proposed System

TrustScope Architecture

The proposed system TrustScope integrates:

🧠
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
⛓️
Blockchain Technology
🔐
Cryptographic Hashing (SHA-256)
🌐
Secure Web Application

The system verifies documents by generating a unique digital fingerprint and storing verification records in a tamper-proof blockchain ledger.
